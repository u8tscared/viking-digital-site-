Multi-Site Nordic Platform

A diversified portfolio of campuses across Norway and Finland, enabling scalable and flexible infrastructure deployment.

Large-Scale Development Capability

Designed to support high-capacity, phased build-outs aligned with long-term hyperscale, AI and cloud demand.

Renewable Power Foundations

Built around access to predominantly renewable energy for reliable, sustainable and low-carbon operations.

AI-Ready Campus Design

Engineered for next-generation compute, including high-density, liquid-cooled GPU and TPU environments.

Our portfolio is structured as a series of strategically located campuses across the Nordics. Each site has been selected based on power availability, cooling efficiency, connectivity potential and long-term scalability.

The platform is designed to support phased development, allowing capacity to be brought online in line with customer demand while maintaining financial prudence and flexibility.

Site selection

A disciplined approach to site selection.

Every project meets the requirements of next-generation compute and long-term scalability.

Power-First Strategy

Sites are prioritised based on access to reliable, renewable power and grid infrastructure.

Cooling Efficiency

Locations maximise the use of natural climate and, where possible, access to cold coastal water.

Scalability

Campuses are designed for phased expansion, allowing infrastructure to grow over time.

Connectivity Potential

Sites are aligned with fibre routing and access to major European network corridors.

Phased Development Model

From land readiness to live compute.

This phased approach allows Viking Digital Campus to deliver infrastructure efficiently while maintaining flexibility and financial discipline.

  1. 01

    Site Preparation

    Land, infrastructure planning and permitting readiness.

  2. 02

    Power & Infrastructure Alignment

    Grid integration, site servicing and technical preparation.

  3. 03

    Campus Delivery

    Core and shell construction aligned with customer demand.

  4. 04

    Operational Infrastructure

    Deployment of compute environments and long-term operations.
